Applied Physics Formulations (Slides 10 & 13)

1. Image Formation Model (Slide 13):

I(x) = J(x)·t(x) + B·(1 - t(x))

Direct scene attenuation plus ambient water backscatter veil.

2. Optimization Problem (Slide 13):

J* = argmin ½||I - (J·t + B(1-t))||² + λR(J) + μL_det(J)

Balances physics data fidelity, regularizer prior, and detector feedback.

3. One Unrolled Stage k (Slide 13):

Z_k = J_{k-1} - ρ_k·∇f(J_{k-1})

J_k = ProxNet_k(Z_k, F_det)

Learned proximal operator substitutes handcrafted regularizer with detector guidance.
